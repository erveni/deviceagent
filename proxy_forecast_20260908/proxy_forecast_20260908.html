What work is included?

Daily: 30 runs a month

Our normal nightly sessions use about 8.54 GB per night. Repeating that for 30 nights gives us 256.11 GB a month.

This comes from a measured night, not a 30-day average. Busy nights can cost more.

Ranking: two full sweeps a month

A sweep means completing 3,492 keyword-and-platform checks. Two sweeps mean 6,984 successful checks, using an estimated 365.35 GB a month at the old measured workflow rate.

One keyword checked on Gemini is one pair. The same keyword checked on ChatGPT is another. Two sweeps is a planning assumption, not a confirmed delivery schedule.

Where we can reduce the bill

Measured in a small test

Stop downloading the same Gemini files every time

Keeping reusable browser files while clearing the old conversation reduced the observed cost from 20.97 MB to 4.12 MB per successful ranking in our recent comparison.

The pilot spent 24.71 MB in total. Six jobs succeeded, one was interrupted, and three did not start. The bill includes the interrupted attempt.

That is promising. But the test used one phone with an already warmed cache, and ordinary browser clears can remove those savings. We have not rolled this out across the farm.

Not deducted from this budget

Why the forecast still uses the old ranking cost

The old production sample cost 52.31 MB per successful pair across platforms, including failed work and retries. The 20.97 MB comparison was a different, single successful Gemini job.

We cannot replace the whole farm's 52.31 MB rate with a six-success Gemini pilot. The forecast keeps the production baseline until the broader test is complete.

If 1,000 comparable Gemini successes reproduce the 20.97 → 4.12 MB result, the saving would be about 16.85 GB, or $8.26 at Evomi's extra-GB rate. This is an illustration, not a committed monthly saving.

  1. Fix screenshot problems without asking for a new answer

    We have recovered complete screenshots from the answer already on screen. That avoids paying for another generation. We still need to measure the remaining screenshot work, and we must keep the checks that reject missing or incorrect ranks.

  2. Use ZIP targeting only when the job needs it

    A small city-first test cost less per successful result than ZIP-first routing. We need to verify location accuracy before changing client work. Evomi charges extra bandwidth for ZIP targeting; DataImpulse doubles the rate for state, city or ZIP targeting.

  3. Stop repeating failures that are unlikely to recover

    Repeated timeouts and broken input steps spend data without producing a result. Track them by phone and failure type. Simply turning all retries off is not the answer: one test used less total bandwidth but cost more per successful result.

  4. Keep the daily's reliability fixes

    Keep Copilot capped at four jobs at once and keep its Edge reset. Test any lighter reset separately. A 10% daily bandwidth reduction would save about $12.55 a month on Evomi, but we have not achieved that saving.

  5. Do free diagnostics first; measure setup before optimizing it

    Local screenshot inspection, OCR and APK transfers do not need the paid proxy. We should also measure repeated proxy preflight checks and background downloads. So far, measured idle and warmup traffic is small; leaving the proxy connected longer is not the main proven cause of the high ranking bill.

These opportunities overlap. The Gemini pilot used both caching and screenshot recovery, so we cannot add their savings together as if they were separate.

What could change these numbers?

How much ranking we run

On Evomi, daily plus one full sweep is about $215.99/month. Two sweeps brings it to $305.50. A steady 14-day cycle averages about $318.29 over a 30-day month.

Clearing the last recorded backlog of 2,108 pairs would add roughly 110.27 GB, or $54.03 in Evomi overage. Check the actual remaining queue first, and do not add this again if a planned sweep already includes it.

Our actual plan and success rate

Confirm account discounts, taxes and targeting charges before buying. Then compare providers on successful, correctly located results, not just advertised price per GB.

The estimate uses decimal GB (1,000 MB). It assumes the measured night repeats and the farm can deliver the planned ranking volume. We should replace those assumptions with a full month of usage as we collect it.
